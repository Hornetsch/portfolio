about the artist

A hornet, a pencil, and far too much enthusiasm

Hi, i’m Hornetsch. I draw characters with a lot of feeling in them; bright cel shading, silly expressions, and the occasional dramatic sunset. My favourite subjects are my own cast of critters, whatever Pokémon has taken over my brain that week, and teams of very serious heroes who are absolutely not serious at all.
